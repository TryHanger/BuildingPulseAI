# BP-ML-REPRO-03 daily coverage policy

Policy ID `sat-daily-coverage@1.0.0`. Locked before any BP-ML-REPRO-03 fault inference. This engineering floor is predeclared on the minute sampling cadence and operating regime. It has no field calibration or statistical guarantee. REPRO-01 and REPRO-02 receipts retain their own historical any-eligible-minute policy.

- Use source-clock calendar dates (00:00 inclusive, next 00:00 exclusive). The day has 1,440 nominal minute slots and eligibility still uses the frozen 300 s fan-on hold with reset across gaps. Only RUNNING enters the score. OFF and warm-up contribute zero scored minutes. An entire day without usable minutes returns `NOT_EVALUABLE`.
- For a daily result require at least 120 unique usable minutes, a run of at least 30 contiguous usable minutes, and usable minutes covering at least 80% of the expected RUNNING minutes. Expected RUNNING minutes are obtained independently of residuals from the fan status and the source clock. For archive scoring require the complete fan timeline; unknown fan evidence on that timeline returns `NOT_EVALUABLE`. Retain the already pinned archive eligibility receipt when selecting prediction inputs. For matched pairs apply the 80% requirement independently to both sides against their own RUNNING denominators.
- A nonempty day below any floor is `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`. Report usable and RUNNING minutes, daily calendar coverage and continuous run length. Minute points can still be shown for insufficient coverage. Missing required channels and invalid calibration abstain. Duplicate, out-of-order or off-grid minutes fail closed. The historical threshold remains strict `z > 3`; abstention produces neither a daily score nor a daily alarm.
- Study outputs keep both historical-mask scores and the new coverage-policy result. Recompute native SAT, native eight-target maximum and SAT−setpoint on the exact healthy/fault minute intersection as a separate analysis. Use exported Ridge and PCA scores only on dates where timestamp-set equality makes them valid for that intersection; unavailable matched scores stay blank. BASIC without commands is a separate historical ablation because that native arm was never published in the pilot.
